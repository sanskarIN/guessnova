# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on the work. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Respect privacy and do not publish private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report project conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Reports should include enough context to investigate while avoiding unnecessary sensitive information.
